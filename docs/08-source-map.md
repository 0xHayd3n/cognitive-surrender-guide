## Reference-list roadmap

The bibliography connects dual-process families and critiques (Kahneman, Stanovich/West, Sloman, Epstein, Evans, Keren/Schul, Melnikoff/Bargh), distributed/external cognition (Clark/Chalmers, Wegner, Risko/Gilbert), automation and algorithm advice (Mosier/Skitka, Dietvorst and colleagues, Logg and colleagues), CRT and decision measures (Frederick, Manfredi/Nave, NFC and intelligence sources), incentives/time constraints, and emerging AI applications. It also cites the earlier System 0 work of Chiriatti and colleagues. (pp. 51–58.)

These citations provide intellectual context. The guide has read their entries in this manuscript, not independently verified every cited study. Do not present a cited third-party result as an experiment conducted by Shaw and Nave or by this repository. For detailed claims about another study, consult and cite that study directly.
